Developer news strip on the Creatio login page

A 32 px line under the profile selector. It cannot be hidden, but it stays quiet until there is something new. Content comes from a remote JSON feed, so publishing news needs no extension release. Spec: docs/features/developer-news.md.

Live mockup

Click the strip to expand the feed. Switch scenarios on the right. News texts are placeholders, not real announcements.
