## edgeBlob.js

**Purpose:** Lets the user hide the floating buttons as a small black blob stuck to a screen edge. Pushing the buttons past an edge first shows a black wet spot there; pushed further, the buttons turn into the blob without the mouse being released. The buttons stay docked until the blob is pulled away from the edge further than a button is high; that tears it off into the buttons. A click on the blob, or resting the mouse on it for 2 s, only brings the buttons out for a while (a "peek"): they sit in a pool that grows out of the blob, and go back into the blob 10 s after the mouse left them and their menus, or at once on a click anywhere else on the page. Dragging the buttons out of the pool further than a button is high tears the pool and frees them for good; a shorter drag puts them back.

**Public API:**
- `PRESS_DISTANCE` (20 px), `EDGE_HOLD_MS` (600 ms), `TEAR_DISTANCE` (36 px, fallback when the button height is unknown), `PEEK_HOVER_MS` (2000 ms), `PEEK_IDLE_MS` (10 000 ms), `EDGE_GAP` (8 px), `DOT_FLIGHT_MS` (420 ms), `EDGES`
- `pressedEdge(rawX, rawY, width, height, vw, vh): { edge, over } | null` — which edge an unclamped box is pushed past and by how much
- `isCursorAtEdge(edge, x, y, vw, vh): boolean`
- `clampAlong(edge, along, vw, vh)`, `alongForRect(edge, rect, vw, vh)` — blob centre along the edge, kept on screen
- `bellHeight(d)` — how far the resting blob stands out from the edge `d` px from its middle
- `buttonsPositionFor(edge, along, width, height, vw, vh, gap?)` — where the buttons stand when they come out of the blob
- `saveBlobState(pageType, edge, along)`, `loadBlobState(pageType, cb)`, `clearBlobState(pageType)` — `chrome.storage.local` key `menuEdgeBlob_{pageType}_{origin}`
- `colorLuminance(css)` — relative luminance of a computed `rgb()`/`rgba()` colour, `null` for other or mostly transparent colours
- `gradientLuminance(image)` — mean luminance of the colours of a CSS gradient, `null` without one
- `isDarkAt(x, y, own)` — whether the page under the point is dark (background colour or gradient), ignoring elements inside `own`
- `SHADE_WATCH_MS` (20 s), `SHADE_SETTLE_MS` (0.4 s) — how long a restored blob watches the page, and how long the page must be still before the background is checked again
- `shadeAt(x, y, own)` → `{ dark, by }` — `isDarkAt` plus which element decided it
- `POOL_PAD` (6 px), `POOL_R` (13 px), `DOME_SHAPE` — the pool around the buttons and the dome of the resting blob as a pool shape
- `poolShapeFor(edge, box, vw, vh, pad?)` → `{ c, half, depth }` — pool around a box of buttons: centre along the edge, half length, depth from the edge to `pad` past their far side
- `poolHeight(d, half, depth)`, `poolPath(edge, shape, vw, vh)` — the outline of a pool and its SVG path on the screen
- `viewportWidth()`, `viewportHeight()` — the viewport without scrollbars (`documentElement.clientWidth/Height`)
- `createEdgeBlob({ root, container, pageType, signal, tearDistance?, iconDot?, onBlob, onPeek, onPeekEnd, onTearOff, onUndock, onSnapBack, buttons? })` → `{ edge, peeking, press(container, rawBox, clientX, clientY), release(), dock(edge, along), dragButtons(box), releaseButtons(), uncoverButtons() }`. `onPeek` returns the box of the buttons it placed; `dragButtons` returns `true` once the pool tore

**Key decisions:**
- The drag code stays in `floatingContainer.js`; this module only receives the unclamped box of the buttons on every move. That keeps one drag implementation and makes the edge logic a pure function (`pressedEdge`) that is unit tested without a browser.
- A cursor pinned to the screen edge sends no more mouse moves, so a grab close to the edge could never reach the press distance. Holding the cursor there for `EDGE_HOLD_MS` also turns the buttons into a blob; the hold is timed with `setTimeout`, not on moves.
- The blob and the wet spot are drawn in one left-edge frame and rotated for the other edges (right 180°, top 90°, bottom −90°). The goo look is an SVG filter (blur + alpha threshold) over plain divs; the drops double as the neck while the blob is stretched.
- A blob restored on page load (`dock`) slides out of the edge with a small overshoot and the orange dot fades in (there are no buttons on screen for it to fly from). Without motion it popped up a second after the page opened and was easy to miss; the splat of a new blob, with its flying drops, was too busy for a page that is still loading.
- The blob, the wet spot and the pool are one flat colour (`BLOB_COLOR`, `BLOB_COLOR_ON_DARK` on a dark page), no gradients: a black-to-grey gradient read as two colours.
- Edges are measured against the viewport without scrollbars, so a blob on the right edge of a page with a scrollbar is not hidden under it. Layers: pool 9996 < buttons 9997 < blob and wet spot 9998 < menus 9999 < flying dot 10000, high enough for Creatio's own fixed panels along the edges not to cover the blob.
- On a dark page the black blob is hard to see, so whenever it is shown or moved (`placeBlob`) the background under its middle is checked (`isDarkAt`: the topmost page element there with a background colour, skipping the extension's own elements; luminance below `DARK_LUMINANCE`) and the blob gets `csl-edge-on-dark`. The page is first hit-tested (`elementsFromPoint`); an `<img>` there counts by its own pixel at that point (read through a 1×1 canvas; skipped when transparent there or from another origin). When nothing with a background is hit, the elements under the point are looked up by their boxes (`elementsAtByBox`, a few levels below `body`, topmost first), because on a live Creatio page the check once found only `body` (white) under a dark header, and an element with `pointer-events: none` is left out of hit testing exactly like that (the likely cause; not caught in the act). Colours other than `rgb()` (`oklch()`, `color(srgb …)`, names) are read by painting them on a 1×1 canvas; a `background-image` gradient counts by the mean of its colours. Besides show and move, it is checked when the mouse enters the blob and after a restore on page load or a move to another page of Creatio (`hashchange`): for `SHADE_WATCH_MS` a `MutationObserver` on the page (ignoring the extension's own changes) re-checks the background each time the page has been still for `SHADE_SETTLE_MS`, so the last check sees the settled page, not a loading overlay; then the watch stops. Creatio draws its dark header (a gradient on `div.mask-wrap` under an `img.mountains-image`) 1–3 s after the blob appears and keeps redrawing the card for a while; fixed re-checks at set times turned the blob grey and then black again. No polling. The element that decided the colour is written to `data-shade` on the blob, to check it on a live page. The colour change is a 0.4 s transition, which turns it dark grey (#555 → #2a2a2a). It is not checked on a timer: the page under a blob that stands still rarely changes, and polling costs work on every page. Images, canvases and iframes are not looked into, so the blob stays black over them.
- During a peek the blob turns into a pool (`.csl-edge-pool`, one viewport-sized SVG under the buttons) that grows out of the dome to `POOL_PAD` around the buttons and shrinks back into the dome when the peek ends; then the blob takes its place without a splat. Users did not see that the buttons belong to the blob and will go back into it; buttons sitting inside it make that plain. The pool is built like the bell — a box rounded by `POOL_R` merged with a thin skirt by a smooth union of distance fields — so it flows into the edge like the resting blob, and with `DOME_SHAPE` (half 0, depth 20) it is the dome itself: growing and shrinking are a morph of `{ c, half, depth }`, the path rebuilt on every animation frame (`morphPool`, cancelled through `poolGen`). While the pool grows or shrinks, the buttons (`buttons()`, the wrapper — not the padded container, which the page stylesheets pad and which can lie elsewhere) are clipped to the pool on every frame (`clipToPool`: `inset()` between the ends of the pool along the edge and up to its depth), so on every edge they come out of the blob and go back into it inside the pool, never sticking out of it. `uncoverButtons()` starts the clip and returns a handle whose `onfinish` runs when they are fully out; the buttons get `data-edge-docked` only when the pool has carried them back into the dome. The pool is built around the box of the buttons themselves, and the buttons are centred on the blob by that box. The pool takes the blob's `csl-edge-on-dark` colour. Only its path takes pointer events, and it counts as the buttons, so a click on it neither ends the peek nor closes the menus. It is an SVG element, which has no `hidden` property: it is hidden through the `hidden` attribute.
- The buttons out of the blob can be dragged (the drag code in `floatingContainer` calls `dragButtons(box)` instead of `press`): the pool is redrawn around them; once they are further from the edge than `tearDistance()`, the pool tears (`undock`) and runs back into the edge: the blob state is removed, the idle countdown stops and `onUndock` lets the drag go on with free buttons. Released before that, the move along the edge is kept and the move away from it is undone: `releaseButtons` moves the blob (`along`, saved) to the new spot of the buttons, the pool follows and `onSnapBack` brings the buttons back to the edge there. Moving along the edge does not count towards the tear.
- A peek keeps `edge` and the stored state: docking is permanent until a tear-off or the pool tears. Users found it annoying that the first click on the blob undocked the buttons for good, so a click (mousedown + mouseup without a 4 px move) or a 2 s hover only peeks. While the mouse rests on the blob the ring around the orange dot grows (`csl-edge-charging`) so the hover has visible feedback.
- The 10 s countdown runs only while the mouse is off the extension: a `mouseover` anywhere inside `root` except the blob (the buttons, their menus, the news flyout) pauses it, and leaving restarts it from 10 s. Opening a menu therefore never collapses it under the cursor.
- A press anywhere outside `root` while the buttons are out ends the peek at once (same squash into the blob as after the 10 s). It listens to `mousedown` in the capture phase, so Creatio handlers that stop the event cannot keep the buttons out; presses on the buttons, their menus or the blob do not count.
- The tear distance is a callback (`tearDistance()`), because the button height is known only to the floating container and differs between pages.
- `blobGen` is bumped whenever the blob is shown or hidden; animation `onfinish` handlers check it so a hide that ends after the blob came back (or a squash that ends after a peek started) does nothing.
- Only the mound itself (`.csl-edge-hit`) takes pointer events, not the empty corners of the 36×54 box, so a click next to the blob still reaches Creatio.
- `along` is stored as a fraction of the edge length so the blob keeps its spot after a window resize. The state is per page type and origin, like the drag position, and is removed when the blob becomes buttons again.
- Styles are injected once as `<style id="csl-edge-blob-style">` instead of a manifest stylesheet: the shell and configuration pages load different CSS lists, and the mock e2e pages load none.
- The squash animation marks the buttons `data-edge-docked` only when it ends, and only if that blob still exists: a blob torn off within those 200 ms must not hide the buttons the user is already dragging.
- The orange dot of the blob and the orange dot on the icon of the first button (`.csl-icon-dot`, found through `iconDot()`) are one object for the eye: whenever the blob turns into the buttons (peek, tear-off) a separate `.csl-edge-flydot` flies from one to the other along a small arc, and back when the buttons squash into the blob. Both real dots are hidden during the flight. The target is measured on every animation frame, because the buttons are still popping in or being dragged; a fixed end point would land next to the icon.
- At rest the blob looks like a drop of liquid on a surface: a crisp SVG bell (`.csl-edge-bell`, outline from `bellHeight(d)`: a circle and a gaussian skirt merged by a smooth union of their distance fields, so the dome is round on top and flows into the skirt through concave fillets) — a dome about 20 px high in the middle of a 100 px long skirt that runs out to nothing along the edge. The goo filter cannot draw such thin tails (its threshold eats thin parts and rounds the ends), so the goo shapes (base, core, drops) are kept small inside the bell and only show when the blob is pulled: they make the neck and the core that follows the mouse, while the bell gathers towards the neck. The goo frame (80×160) is centred in the taller 36×100 blob box (`GOO_TOP`).
- `Element.animate` is missing in jsdom; the `animate()` helper applies the end state at once there.

**Dependencies:** `debug.js`
